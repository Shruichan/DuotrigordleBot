Duotrigordle Bot

runs in your browser — no setup

Open duotrigordle.com and the bot overlay appears automatically with its suggested guess.

The first suggestion on a fresh page takes a couple of seconds while the solver warms up its tables; after that it's instant.

Strategy: entropy-greedy across all 32 boards with a value-net tie-break that bounds the worst case (caps games at 35, no 36s). Plays the daily in ~33.6 guesses on average.
