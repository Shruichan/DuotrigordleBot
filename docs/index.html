How well does it play?

2000-game benchmark on the daily answer distribution. Two operating points on one risk dial.

strategymeanmax33s34s35s36s
Tail-averse default 33.76355481382670
Lowest-mean greedy 33.62368311071873

Mean and tail trade off against each other: the value-net tie-break gives up a sliver of average (+0.14) to erase every 36-guess game and trim the 35s. The remaining ~3% of 35s are structural — some daily answer sets force several simultaneous coin-flips no strategy can dodge.

How it works

One guess is scored against all 32 boards together.

01

Entropy across boards

Each candidate word is scored by how much it splits the remaining answer sets — summed over every unsolved board.

02

Solve bonus

A weighted bonus rewards words likely to outright solve a board, so the bot banks boards instead of only gathering information.

03

Value-net tie-break

Near-tied top candidates are re-ranked by a tiny neural net that predicts remaining turns — the risk-averse pick bounds the worst case.

04

Endgame guards

Distinct-answer constraints and a rhyme-trap override keep single-board 50/50s from blowing up into long tails.

Install the extension

Auto-detects the board on duotrigordle.com and overlays the next guess. Chrome / Edge / Brave.

01

Get the files

Download the repo and find the extension/ folder (GitHub).

02

Load unpacked

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click “Load unpacked,” and pick the extension/ folder.

03

Play

Open duotrigordle.com — the overlay shows the suggested guess. First pick takes a couple seconds while the solver warms up.
